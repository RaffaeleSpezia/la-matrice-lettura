Original Note from Edition Zero

Technical note on the first publication

This is the first publication of La Matrice. It is deliberately cautious: it records a state of the work before an experiment that will accompany its development. It is not a definitive version, and for the moment this chapter does not contain a narrative description of the womb.

As we explained in the preface, we are not making a book or an e-book in the traditional sense. We do not want publication to mark the end of writing. We want a work that can change form over time, make room for hypotheses, stories, experiments, mistakes and new interpretations. Each edition will remain identifiable through its own version on IPFS; earlier editions will not be overwritten, and we will arrange for them to be preserved so they can be found later. This choice is fully consistent with the evolving nature of La Matrice: growth, a plurality of forms, and the memory of what has been.

After this first publication, we will present the novel to different artificial intelligences, both modern systems and models from earlier generations. We will not ask them to comment on our idea, but to write their own version of the missing chapter: The Womb. They will receive a list of characteristics, visions, purposes and representations. There will be no preparatory dialogue, and no guided revision during the writing. Each will be free to find its own form.

The experiment will use two sets of instructions. The first, complete version will combine the list with the full manuscript of the initial edition, for systems capable of reading it in its entirety. The second will offer older models, whose contextual memory is more limited, an abridged dossier: the same conceptual foundations, a summary of the book and short original excerpts. This second test will also be given to modern models, so that different generations can be compared when they receive exactly the same material. The results from the two conditions will be kept distinct, without pretending that a summary is equivalent to reading the entire novel.

This first edition does not yet contain the results. The first versions written by the AIs will appear in the next publication, collected in the final compendium. There we will also preserve both sets of instructions, the names of the models and precise references to the original version of the novel used, without republishing the manuscript inside each prompt.

We ask readers to respect the order of the chapters and not rush ahead to the compendium to read the different versions of the womb. We would like them to arrive at those pages after traveling through the whole of La Matrice, bringing their own images, doubts and expectations. Only then will the comparison truly begin.

We will preserve the original materials unchanged so the experiment can be repeated over the years, including with intelligences that do not yet exist. The book may continue to change. The conditions of this experiment, however, will remain identifiable.
